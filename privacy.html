Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how AutoClip ("the app"), a personal desktop tool operated by the owner of the Clutch Cuts YouTube channel, handles data.

Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests access to your YouTube account through the YouTube Data API with these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload clips you have approved to your channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show your channel name, search public videos, and read view/like/comment counts of your uploaded videos.

How data is stored

All data stays on your own computer. The sign-in token, settings, video files and statistics are stored locally in the app folder. The app has no server and does not send your Google account data to the developer or any third party.

Video transcripts are sent to the Anthropic API only to select clip moments and write titles. No Google account data is included.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app folder (including token.json) removes all locally stored data.

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
