AutoClip

A personal desktop tool used by the Clutch Cuts YouTube channel.

AutoClip helps the channel owner turn long-form videos into short vertical clips (YouTube Shorts). It finds suitable source videos (Creative Commons or used with permission), creates captioned clips, lets the owner review and approve every clip, and then uploads approved clips to the owner's own YouTube channel.

How it uses YouTube

  • Uploads videos that the channel owner has approved to the owner's own channel.
  • Searches public videos and reads public video statistics to find source material and to measure how the channel's own Shorts perform.

AutoClip runs on the owner's computer. It is not offered to the public.
